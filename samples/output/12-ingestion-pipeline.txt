Q: how do I purify water and how long do I wait?
A: To purify water using the Life Guard X Emergency Survival Kit, follow these steps:

1. Locate the portable water filter in the kit.
2. Extend the tubing and securely attach it to the water source, ensuring a tight seal.
3. Pump the handle to force water through the filtration membrane, producing clean drinking water.
4. For additional purification, use the water purification tablets by placing the appropriate number of tablets into the water to be treated.
5. Wait for the recommended time before consuming the water (the exact waiting time is provided in the instructions included with the tablets).

Always follow the instructions provided with the water purification system for proper use.
